Sawing pattern

Sawing model: the cant is resawn into core boards of the core board thickness (any remainder above/below the cant is taken as side boards); side boards (above/below the cant and on both flanks) are sawn to the side board thickness and edged to the cant width, so no side board is wider than the cant. Boards with wane (corners outside the log) are allowed and their volume is clipped to the log. Through-and-through sawing uses the core board thickness for all boards. Assumptions: circular cross-section; taper handled by using the mean-section radius for board areas and the small end for layout; no within-zone MC gradient; boards run full length. The density inputs give the SPECIES minimum and maximum oven-dry density (oven-dry mass / oven-dry volume); within each log the radial density range is 0.7 × the species range, placed randomly inside the species range for every log, so all densities stay within the species bounds. Heartwood and sapwood MC vary between logs with SD = 5 % of the given values. The heartwood fraction is sampled 1000 times (one sample per simulated log) from a normal distribution centred on (min + max)/2 with SD = (max − min)/4, kept within [min, max]; sampling is seeded and therefore reproducible. Expected accuracy roughly ±10–15 % on MC — for charge planning and logistics, not payment grading. Calibrate species values against your own mill measurements. Numeric inputs accept both "." and "," as decimal separator.
